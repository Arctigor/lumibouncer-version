LumiBouncer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how LumiBouncer handles information. It applies to the LumiBouncer game for Android and other supported platforms.

Who is responsible

LumiBouncer is published by Arctigor. For privacy questions or requests, contact beneszabi51@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

Game progress stored on your device

LumiBouncer saves game progress locally on your device in a SQLite database. This includes gems, completed and unlocked levels, owned and selected cosmetics, inventory, daily reward claims, and local dates and active-play time used to calculate rewards. The game does not upload this progress to a LumiBouncer account or server; LumiBouncer has no account system or cloud-save service.

You can remove this local data by clearing LumiBouncer's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The Android version integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner and optional rewarded ads. The current project build uses Google's test ad unit IDs. The SDK and Google may receive information needed to make and measure ad requests, such as the device's advertising identifier where available, IP address, device and app information, diagnostics, and ad views or interactions. Google may use or share this information with its service providers and advertising partners under Google's policies. Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address; LumiBouncer does not request precise location.

LumiBouncer is intended for users aged 3 and older, including children. The current project uses test ads and does not implement an age screen or an in-app advertising consent flow. Production advertising must not be enabled until the publisher has configured child-directed treatment, age-appropriate ad content, and any required consent and family-policy settings for the intended audience. Actual data handled by Google depends on the ad configuration and the user's region and device settings. We do not operate Google's ad systems or control their retention practices.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and information about Google's advertising technologies. Users and parents or guardians can review Google's available ad and privacy controls at My Ad Center and in device settings.

Version check

When the game starts, it makes an HTTPS request to GitHub to check whether a newer version is available. The request includes a timestamp to avoid a cached response, but does not include game progress or an account identifier. GitHub may receive the IP address, request metadata, and standard server logs associated with that request. If you choose to open an update page, it opens outside the game; the destination site's own privacy policy then applies.

See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

How information is used and shared

Locally stored game data is used only to save and restore your progress and calculate in-game rewards. We do not sell personal information, run our own analytics service, or receive the locally stored progress described above.

Information sent through the advertising SDK is handled by Google and, as applicable, its service providers and advertising partners to provide, measure, and protect advertising services. The version-check request is handled by GitHub. These third parties process information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law; LumiBouncer does not maintain a user database from which to retrieve game progress.

Retention and your choices

Game progress remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall LumiBouncer. We do not retain a separate copy. Google and GitHub determine the retention of information they receive through their services; use their privacy controls or contact them directly for requests concerning that information.

You can stop using ads by not opening rewarded ads; banner ads may appear in the Android app. Device-level advertising controls may limit advertising personalization, subject to Google's settings and applicable requirements. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the locally saved game progress.

Children and parents or guardians

LumiBouncer is intended for a broad audience that includes children. The game does not require an account and does not ask players to provide their name, email address, contacts, photos, or other personal information. Game progress is stored locally as described above. The advertising SDK may process device and ad information as described in the Advertising section.

Parents or guardians with a privacy concern can contact us at beneszabi51@gmail.com. Requests about information processed by Google should also be directed to Google through its privacy controls and policies.

Security

Progress is stored in the app's private storage on the device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot control the security practices of Google, GitHub, or other services they use.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the URL linked from the app and store listing, with its revision date shown above.
